A little about me

The short version

I try to move through life — and work — with kindness first. I believe good things tend to come back around, so I hold onto patience even when things move slowly, and I stay curious about people, places, and ideas that are different from my own.

Professionally, that shows up as someone who learns quickly, cares about the details, and genuinely enjoys the process of figuring things out. I'm drawn to work that means something, and to teams where relationships matter as much as results.

Outside of work, I'm usually planning my next trip, picking up a new interest, or simply sitting somewhere quiet with a good cup of chai.
